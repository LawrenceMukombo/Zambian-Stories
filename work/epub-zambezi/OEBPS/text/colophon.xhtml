Heritage & Cultural Acknowledgments

This collection draws reverent inspiration from the oral traditions of the BaTonga, Toka-Leya, Aushi, Chishinga, and Kunda peoples of Zambia. For centuries, these legends were passed through firelight storytelling (Insaka), teaching children that human life is intimately woven with sacred rivers, towering trees, and celestial wonders.

The illustrations in this edition are crafted in an authentic hand-drawn storybook style, combining textured pencil linework with organic watercolor washes that celebrate the warmth and beauty of the Zambian landscape.
