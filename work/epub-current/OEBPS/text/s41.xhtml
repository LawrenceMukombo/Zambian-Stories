LANSDOWN COLLECTION 1

Why the Lion Roars

Long ago Lion's voice was as quiet as that of a lamb. This allowed him to catch and eat the other animals without warning. One day all the animals gathered to find a way to stop his cruelty. They talked for a long time but found no answer.

At last Hare had a clever idea. "I shall change his voice into a mighty roar," he said. "Then we will always know when he is coming." The animals agreed, and Hare began his difficult task. He went to Lion and said, "My lord Lion, someone is very ill and wishes to see you."

"Where is he?" Lion asked. "Follow me and I will take you to him," Hare replied. Hare led Lion deep into the bush. Lion became tired and lay down beneath a tree, where he soon fell asleep. A hive of bees was nearby. Hare took honey from it and smeared it all over Lion's body while he slept.

When the bees returned and saw that their honey had been stolen, they became furious. They found Lion covered in honey and attacked him. Their stings caused such terrible pain that Lion awoke with a thunderous roar. He roared so much that his gentle voice was changed forever.

To this day the animals thank Hare for his cleverness, for whenever Lion approaches they hear his roar and run away.

Lesson from the Story

Stories can explain the world while teaching careful choices.

Knowledge Check / Think About It

1. What problem begins Why the Lion Roars?

A. The animals wanted Lion to sing.

B. Lion could catch animals because his voice was quiet.

C. Lion had lost his home.

Answer: B

2. What choice changes Why the Lion Roars?

A. Hare covered Lion with honey so bees would sting him.

B. Hare gave Lion a crown.

C. Hare hid Lion in a cave.

Answer: A

3. What lesson can readers learn from Why the Lion Roars?

A. Quiet voices are always dangerous.

B. Bees should never guard honey.

C. Cleverness should protect others from cruelty.

Answer: C
