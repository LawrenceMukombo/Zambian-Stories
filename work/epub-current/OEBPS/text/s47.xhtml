LANSDOWN COLLECTION 7

How Hippo Lost His Horns

Many years ago, when Hippo lived on dry land, he was a very handsome animal. He had magnificent golden horns, long and beautifully curved like those worn in the ancient land of Persia. All the animals wished for horns like his, except one jealous little creature with a very long nose.

That animal decided to trick him. One day he found Hippo and said, "Master Hippo, today is very hot and you are sweating. I do not think you dare to bathe in that pool, because you are afraid the gate that holds your horns may not keep them in place beneath the water."

Hippo replied proudly, "My horns are perfectly secure." At once Hippo plunged into the cool pool. The water was so refreshing that he dived and rolled about in it. But the water loosened his horns and washed them away'splash!

Ashamed and angry, he remained in the pool to hide. Thus the long-nosed animal outwitted him. To this day Hippo lives in cool river water, still mourning the beautiful horns he lost.

Lesson from the Story

Pride can make a person easy to trick.

New Words

  • Magnificent: very beautiful and impressive
  • Jealous: unhappy because someone else has something good
  • Outwitted: defeated by clever thinking

Knowledge Check / Think About It

1. What problem begins How Hippo Lost His Horns?

A. Hippo refused to drink water.

B. Hippo was proud of his golden horns.

C. Hippo wanted to become a bird.

Answer: B

2. What choice changes How Hippo Lost His Horns?

A. The water washed his horns away.

B. Hare hid the horns in a basket.

C. The horns became trees.

Answer: A

3. What lesson can readers learn from How Hippo Lost His Horns?

A. Beautiful things can never be lost.

B. Pools are always unsafe.

C. Pride can make someone easy to trick.

Answer: C
