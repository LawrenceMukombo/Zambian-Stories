LANSDOWN COLLECTION 12

Why the Owl Flies at Night

Many years ago all birds and animals believed Owl to be extremely wise. Whenever a dispute arose, they brought it to Owl so that he might judge it. One day Hare asked him, "Master Owl, which is the wiser: a white chicken or a black one?"

Owl answered, "That is easy. The black chicken is wiser, because it can lay a white egg, while the white chicken cannot lay a black egg." "Thank you, Master Owl," said Hare. "If you are so wise, can you lay a black egg?"

Owl confidently said that he could and went away to try. But no matter how hard he tried, only a white egg appeared. Deeply ashamed, Owl thought, "How can I face the animals after claiming that I could lay a black egg?" He hid until darkness fell.

Since that day Owl has flown only at night, hiding his embarrassment from the other animals.

Lesson from the Story

Stories can explain the world while teaching careful choices.

Knowledge Check / Think About It

1. What problem begins Why the Owl Flies at Night?

A. Owl promised to teach Fish.

B. Owl stole a needle.

C. Owl claimed he could lay a black egg.

Answer: C

2. What choice changes Why the Owl Flies at Night?

A. He was looking for honey.

B. He was ashamed when he could not do it.

C. He wanted to hunt at night.

Answer: B

3. What lesson can readers learn from Why the Owl Flies at Night?

A. False claims of wisdom can bring shame.

B. Judges must always hide.

C. Black eggs solve every dispute.

Answer: A
