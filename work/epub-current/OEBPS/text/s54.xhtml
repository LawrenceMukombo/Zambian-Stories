LANSDOWN COLLECTION 14

Why the Cat and Dog Quarrel

Long ago people kept Cat and Dog together in their houses to guard their possessions. One day a man who owned both animals told them to let no one enter while he was away. He left a large piece of fat meat on the table and ordered them to guard it.

As soon as the man had gone, Cat began eating the meat. When the owner returned, it was gone. He asked sternly, "Who ate it?" Cat quickly answered, "I do not know. Perhaps a stranger took it while Dog and I were outside."

Dog became angry at the lie and said, "That is not true. I saw Cat eating it. Look'there are still pieces of meat around his mouth." The owner seized Cat and beat him. Cat was furious with Dog for exposing him. He chased him away, crying, "If I ever see you again, I shall bite you!"

Since that day Cat and Dog have been enemies.

Lesson from the Story

Stories can explain the world while teaching careful choices.

Knowledge Check / Think About It

1. What problem begins Why the Cat and Dog Quarrel?

A. Cat ate the meat he was meant to guard.

B. Dog ate the owner.

C. The owner lost his house.

Answer: A

2. What choice changes Why the Cat and Dog Quarrel?

A. Dog hid the evidence.

B. Dog ran into the forest.

C. Dog told the truth.

Answer: C

3. What lesson can readers learn from Why the Cat and Dog Quarrel?

A. Truth should always be hidden.

B. Lying can break trust and friendship.

C. Guarding means eating first.

Answer: B

Source
