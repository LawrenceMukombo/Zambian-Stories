LANSDOWN COLLECTION 18

Why Monkeys Resemble People

Many years ago no rain fell, and famine came upon the land. The people gathered all the food that remained and hid it in one place, appointing trustworthy men to guard it. One day they discovered the guards eating the food.

Everyone was furious and brought the men before the chief. The chief said, "You will run into the forest in shame because you have stolen our food. I shall not kill you, but I shall punish you in a way you will never forget.

I will turn you into animals that resemble people. Human beings will chase you from their villages, and you will live in trees and eat wild fruit." He changed them into monkeys. Ashamed, they fled into the forest. That is why monkeys resemble human beings.

Lesson from the Story

Stealing from the community brings shame and consequences.

New Words

  • Famine: a time when food is very scarce
  • Trustworthy: able to be trusted
  • Punish: give a consequence for wrongdoing

Knowledge Check / Think About It

1. What problem begins Why Monkeys Resemble People?

A. The chief stole wild fruit.

B. The people refused to gather food.

C. The guards stole the stored food.

Answer: C

2. What choice changes Why Monkeys Resemble People?

A. The chief sent them to the river.

B. The chief changed them into monkeys.

C. The chief gave them crowns.

Answer: B

3. What lesson can readers learn from Why Monkeys Resemble People?

A. Stealing from the community brings shame.

B. Food guards may eat everything.

C. Famine makes stealing right.

Answer: A
