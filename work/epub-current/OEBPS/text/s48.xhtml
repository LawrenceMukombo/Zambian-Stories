Why the Hyena Walks with a Limp

Have you noticed how Hyena seems to limp when he walks? It happened because Hare played a cruel trick on him. One day Hare and Hyena were travelling together. Hyena laughed and said, "How badly you walk! Perhaps your legs are crooked."

Hare pretended to be hurt, then asked, "Will you do something to help me?" "Certainly," Hyena replied. Hare said, "Close your eyes and walk backwards. Continue until I tell you to stop." Foolish Hyena obeyed. Walking backwards with his eyes shut, he fell into a very deep hole.

His legs were badly injured, and from that time he began to limp. He has never walked properly since, because Hare punished him for his mockery.

Lesson from the Story

Stories can explain the world while teaching careful choices.

Knowledge Check / Think About It

1. What problem begins Why the Hyena Walks with a Limp?

A. Hyena mocked Hares walk.

B. Hyena helped Hare build a house.

C. Hyena gave Hare food.

Answer: A

2. What choice changes Why the Hyena Walks with a Limp?

A. Hare told Hyena to sing loudly.

B. Hare told Hyena to count stars.

C. Hare told Hyena to walk backwards with eyes closed.

Answer: C

3. What lesson can readers learn from Why the Hyena Walks with a Limp?

A. Closed eyes make travel safer.

B. Mocking others can bring consequences.

C. Walking backwards is always wise.

Answer: B
