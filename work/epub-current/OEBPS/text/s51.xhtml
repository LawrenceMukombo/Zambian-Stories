LANSDOWN COLLECTION 11

Why Porcupine Has Spikes

Porcupine was once a very proud animal. He was also handsome and loved hearing the other animals praise his beauty. One day he boasted to Hyena, "If every animal were as beautiful as I am, the world would be a splendid place."

Hyena was offended and began thinking of a way to humble him. Hyena said with a laugh, "Listen, sir. In that thorny grove lives someone who can make you even more beautiful. Go and ask him. But first leave your fine coat with me, so that it will not be spoiled."

Foolish Porcupine did as he was told and entered the thorn bushes. The thorns scratched his whole body, and he could not free himself. Hyena laughed and said, "You cannot wear your beautiful coat over those ugly stripes.

I shall wear it myself." So Hyena kept Porcupine’s soft and handsome coat, while Porcupine was left with a rough coat marked by dark stripes. From that day he could boast of his beauty no longer.

Lesson from the Story

Pride in beauty can make someone careless.

New Words

  • Boasted: spoke proudly about oneself
  • Humble: make someone less proud
  • Grove: a small group of trees or bushes

Knowledge Check / Think About It

1. What problem begins Why Porcupine Has Spikes?

A. Porcupine boasted about his beauty.

B. Porcupine lost his way.

C. Porcupine wanted to build a boat.

Answer: A

2. What choice changes Why Porcupine Has Spikes?

A. Lion gave Porcupine a new coat.

B. Owl judged a dispute.

C. Hyena sent Porcupine into thorn bushes.

Answer: C

3. What lesson can readers learn from Why Porcupine Has Spikes?

A. Thorns are good clothing.

B. Pride can make someone careless.

C. Beauty should make someone cruel.

Answer: B
