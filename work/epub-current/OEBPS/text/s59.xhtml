LANSDOWN COLLECTION 19

The Friendship of Pulu and Ngolo

If you travel through the bush, you may see Pulu and Ngolo together, for they are close friends. One day Ngolo asked the animals to help him kill Crocodile, who had eaten many of their companions. The animals refused, saying, "We are afraid of Crocodile."

At last Pulu said, "I will help if you follow my plan." Ngolo asked what he intended to do. Pulu replied, "We shall drink all the water from the pool. Then we will be able to see Crocodile and kill him." Ngolo agreed, and together they began drinking.

Clever Crocodile saw what they were doing, slipped quietly from the shrinking pool and fled toward a large river far away. Pulu and Ngolo followed, caught him, bit him and trampled him again and again. Crocodile's soft skin was badly wounded.

Great rough scales rose wherever they had injured him. He eventually escaped into the river, but his body had been changed. From that day Pulu and Ngolo have remained friends. When they go to drink, they first look carefully around, because Crocodile still waits for a chance to catch them and avenge the damage they did to his skin.

Lesson from the Story

Friends can achieve difficult things when they work together.

New Words

  • Companions: friends or those who travel together
  • Shrinking: becoming smaller
  • Avenge: punish someone for harm done

Knowledge Check / Think About It

1. What problem begins The Friendship of Pulu and Ngolo?

A. Pulu and Ngolo wanted to become birds.

B. Pulu and Ngolo wanted to stop Crocodile.

C. Pulu and Ngolo wanted to build a palace.

Answer: B

2. What choice changes The Friendship of Pulu and Ngolo?

A. They tried to drink all the pool water.

B. They sang until Crocodile slept.

C. They borrowed Hornbills needle.

Answer: A

3. What lesson can readers learn from The Friendship of Pulu and Ngolo?

A. Crocodiles enjoy small pools.

B. Drinking always solves every problem.

C. Friends can work together against danger.

Answer: C

Source
