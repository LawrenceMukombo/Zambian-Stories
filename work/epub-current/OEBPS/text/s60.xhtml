LANSDOWN COLLECTION 20

Why Collared Dove Has a Ring Around His Neck

Chameleon was not always as brightly coloured and beautiful as he is today. One day he saw birds wearing feathers of many colours and wondered where they had obtained them. The birds told him, "Ask Collared dove. He knows where beautiful colours are kept."

Chameleon found Collared dove and asked for help. Collared dove said, "I will help you. Come to me when you see a rainbow, and I will take you there." When a rainbow appeared, Chameleon returned with several beetles who also wanted new colours.

Collared dove said, "Hold on to one another around my neck so that I can carry you to the rainbow." They did so, and Collared dove took them there and rolled upon the colours. Chameleon received many beautiful colours, and so did some of the beetles.

But in their excitement none of them released Collared dove's neck before they left. Their tight grip made a lasting ring around it. Collared dove did not mind, because everyone heard that he had helped Chameleon become beautiful.

Lesson from the Story

Helping others can leave a lasting mark of honour.

New Words

  • Rainbow: coloured arc seen after rain
  • Released: let go
  • Lasting: continuing for a long time

Knowledge Check / Think About It

1. What problem begins Why Collared Dove Has a Ring Around His Neck?

A. Collared Dove carried Chameleon to the rainbow.

B. Owl carried Chameleon at night.

C. Fish carried Chameleon underwater.

Answer: A

2. What choice changes Why Collared Dove Has a Ring Around His Neck?

A. Dove wore a necklace from the chief.

B. A thorn bush scratched Dove.

C. Beetles held tightly around Doves neck.

Answer: C

3. What lesson can readers learn from Why Collared Dove Has a Ring Around His Neck?

A. Colours belong only to birds.

B. Helping others can leave a lasting mark of honour.

C. Rainbows should be kept secret.

Answer: B
