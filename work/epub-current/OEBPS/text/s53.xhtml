LANSDOWN COLLECTION 13

Why Tortoise Has a Hard Back

Tortoise was the last of all the animals to be created. By then the Creator had used nearly every part, so the animals gathered their leftovers for him. They gave him a tongue like a lizard's, a birdlike beak and nose, a head shaped like a snake's, and front legs unlike his hind legs.

Even so, Tortoise was content and lived peacefully among the others. One day Chameleon asked, "Friend, why are you so ugly?" Tortoise replied, "I may look ugly, but my flesh tastes better than that of any other animal." Chameleon could not keep a secret and spread the news everywhere.

When the animals heard that Tortoise had delicious flesh, they began hunting and eating his people. The Tortoise found no escape from the hunters, so they begged a tree to protect them. The tree agreed and grew a hard covering of bark over their bodies.

Thus they gained strong shields. To deceive their enemies, each one travelled alone, pretending that another Tortoise was close behind. Eventually their trickery made them distrust one another, and they scattered. That is why Tortoise has a hard back and why two of them are seldom seen walking together.

Lesson from the Story

Careless words can put others in danger.

Knowledge Check / Think About It

1. What problem begins Why Tortoise Has a Hard Back?

A. The tree chased Tortoise.

B. Chameleon spread Tortoises secret.

C. Tortoise stole Cats meat.

Answer: B

2. What choice changes Why Tortoise Has a Hard Back?

A. A tree grew hard bark over the tortoises.

B. Mouse treated Tortoises wound.

C. Birds gave Tortoise wings.

Answer: A

3. What lesson can readers learn from Why Tortoise Has a Hard Back?

A. Secrets should be shouted in markets.

B. Hard backs come from laziness.

C. Careless words can put others in danger.

Answer: C
