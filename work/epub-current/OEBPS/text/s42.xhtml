Why Ostrich Has a Long Neck

Long ago, before Ostrich had a long neck, Crocodile and Ostrich were friends. The other animals warned Ostrich, It is foolish to be Crocodile’s friend." But Ostrich had a large head and very little sense, and would not listen.

One day Crocodile was extremely hungry, having eaten nothing for many days. He said, "Friend, my throat is sore. Put your head into my mouth and see where it hurts." Foolishly Ostrich obeyed. Crocodile closed his jaws and bit Ostrich’s head.

Ostrich pulled and pulled until at last he escaped, but all that pulling stretched his neck very long. Crocodile finally released him, and Ostrich fled in terror. From that day he has lived only in swamps and small streams, always watching for crocodiles.

Lesson from the Story

Choose friends wisely and listen when others warn you about danger.

New Words

  • Swamps: wet muddy places
  • Foolishly: without good sense
  • Terror: great fear

Knowledge Check / Think About It

1. What problem begins Why Ostrich Has a Long Neck?

A. Crocodile tricked Ostrich into putting his head in his mouth.

B. Ostrich wanted to become a fish.

C. The other animals stole Ostrichs food.

Answer: A

2. What choice changes Why Ostrich Has a Long Neck?

A. A tree pulled Ostrich upward.

B. Ostrich swallowed a long vine.

C. Pulling away from Crocodile stretched Ostrichs neck.

Answer: C

3. What lesson can readers learn from Why Ostrich Has a Long Neck?

A. A big head always means wisdom.

B. Listen to warnings about dangerous friends.

C. Always trust anyone who asks for help.

Answer: B

Source

G. N. Lansdown, Why the Lion Roars and Other Animal Stories from Africa (Macmillan & Co Ltd, 1960; first edition 1955). English rendering from the Luvale edition translated by the Chitokoloki Mission, Balovale.
