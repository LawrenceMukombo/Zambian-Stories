Why Chickens Scratch the Ground

Have you ever wondered why chickens continually scratch the earth? They are always searching for something. This is the reason. Long ago, before the hornbill could fly, he was a close friend of the chickens. One day he obtained a beautiful, valuable needle with which he could sew his clothes.

The chickens saw their friend flying gracefully high in the sky and wanted to do the same. They begged, "Please lend us your needle. We want to make wings like yours." Hornbill answered, "No. You may lose it, and then our friendship will end."

The chickens promised to guard it carefully. At last Hornbill lent them the precious needle and warned them once more not to lose it. Soon one careless chicken used it and misplaced it. Hornbill and all his relatives became angry and began hunting chickens.

"From this day," they said, ?you will be our prey until you find our needle." That is why chickens still fear birds of prey and begin scratching whenever one appears. They are searching for the bright needle that they lost long ago.

Knowledge Check / Think About It

1. What problem begins Why Chickens Scratch the Ground?

A. The chickens borrowed a cooking pot.

B. The chickens borrowed a drum.

C. The chickens borrowed Hornbills needle.

Answer: C

2. What choice changes Why Chickens Scratch the Ground?

A. They are hiding from rain.

B. They are searching for the lost needle.

C. They are planting maize.

Answer: B

3. What lesson can readers learn from Why Chickens Scratch the Ground?

A. Guard borrowed things carefully.

B. Borrowed things never need returning.

C. Friends should ignore promises.

Answer: A
