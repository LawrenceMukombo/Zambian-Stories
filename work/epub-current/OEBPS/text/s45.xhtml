LANSDOWN COLLECTION 5

Why the Hyena Laughs

Long ago Hyena was walking across the plain in high spirits when he met his friend Hare. "Master Hare," Hyena said, ?what news have you for me today?" "Well," Hare replied, closing one eye, "I saw a strange little animal today."

Hyena asked, "What was it like? Did it have a tail or horns?" Hare said, "It had neither, but its legs were extremely long." "My goodness! What did it look like?" cried Hyena. Smiling, Hare continued, "It could change itself into many shapes - a tall person, a short one, a fat one or a thin one."

Hyena stared at the ground and asked, "Were its eyes like ours?" "Not at all, friend," said Hare. "One eye looked straight ahead while the other looked sideways." This description amazed Hyena. He burst into uncontrollable laughter and ran away.

Even today, when you hear Hyena laughing in the night, you may know that he is remembering Hare's absurd animal. Can you guess what creature Hare described? It was a human being!

Lesson from the Story

Stories can explain the world while teaching careful choices.

Knowledge Check / Think About It

1. What problem begins Why the Hyena Laughs?

A. Hare described a human being in a strange way.

B. Hare described a river.

C. Hare described a cooking pot.

Answer: A

2. What choice changes Why the Hyena Laughs?

A. Hyena learned to fly.

B. Hyena became a chief.

C. Hyena laughed uncontrollably.

Answer: C

3. What lesson can readers learn from Why the Hyena Laughs?

A. Strange stories should never be heard.

B. Mockery can make someone look foolish.

C. Laughter always solves danger.

Answer: B

Source
