LANSDOWN COLLECTION 15

Why the Elephant Fears the Mouse

It seems strange that a creature as huge and powerful as Elephant should fear Mouse, but it is true. Some say Elephant thinks Mouse may crawl into his trunk and block it. Here is another explanation. Many years ago Elephant had a painful wound in his leg that would not heal.

One day Mouse found him exhausted with pain. "Master Elephant, what is wrong?" Mouse asked. Elephant told him about the wound. Mouse examined it and said, "I can help you." He began treating and cleaning it. After several days it had still not healed, and Elephant promised to reward Mouse for every object Mouse removed from the wound.

Whenever Mouse asked for payment, Elephant told him to search again, and the treatment continued for a long time. Now whenever Elephant sees Mouse, he becomes afraid, thinking that Mouse has come to demand the reward still owed to him.

Lesson from the Story

Stories can explain the world while teaching careful choices.

Knowledge Check / Think About It

1. What problem begins Why the Elephant Fears the Mouse?

A. Mouse stole Elephants trunk.

B. Elephant chased all doctors away.

C. Mouse treated Elephants wound.

Answer: C

2. What choice changes Why the Elephant Fears the Mouse?

A. Mouse promised to roar.

B. Elephant promised a reward.

C. Elephant promised to become small.

Answer: B

3. What lesson can readers learn from Why the Elephant Fears the Mouse?

A. Promises and debts should not be ignored.

B. Big animals owe nothing.

C. Small helpers cannot matter.

Answer: A
