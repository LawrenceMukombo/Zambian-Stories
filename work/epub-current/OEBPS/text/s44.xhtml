LANSDOWN COLLECTION 4

Why Frog Says Croack-Croack

Long ago Frog spoke as clearly as a bird. One day Frog and Mouse were walking together when they found a cooking rack with a fine piece of meat upon it. Mouse said, "Let us eat it before its owner returns. You climb up and taste it while I watch the path."

"Very well," said Frog, ?but watch carefully and warn me when the owner comes." Frog climbed up, tore off a delicious piece and put it into his mouth. The owner soon approached. Mouse was frightened and ran away without warning his friend.

Frog could think of nothing to do but pretend to be asleep. The meat in his mouth was extremely hot and burned him, yet he dared not chew, spit it out or cry out, for the owner would see him. He held it in his mouth until the owner finished eating and left.

That night Frog tried to speak as before, but the hot meat had badly burned his mouth. He could only make the sound ?mbototo-mbototo." From that day to this, that is all he has been able to say.

Lesson from the Story

A friend should give warning in danger and not abandon another friend.

New Words

  • Rack: a frame used for holding or cooking things
  • Approached: came near
  • Dared: was brave enough to do something

Knowledge Check / Think About It

1. What problem begins Why Frog Says Croack-Croack?

A. The owner invited Frog to eat.

B. Mouse ran away without warning Frog.

C. Frog chased Mouse away.

Answer: B

2. What choice changes Why Frog Says Croack-Croack?

A. Hot meat burned Frogs mouth.

B. Cold water froze Frogs tongue.

C. A bird stole Frogs voice.

Answer: A

3. What lesson can readers learn from Why Frog Says Croack-Croack?

A. A friend should run away silently.

B. A friend should laugh at trouble.

C. A friend should warn another friend in danger.

Answer: C

Source
