LANSDOWN COLLECTION 17

Why Leopard Has Spots

Long ago Leopard had a plain body like Lion's. He was unhappy and wished that his skin could be bright and attractive like that of other animals. He kept this desire hidden in his heart. One day he met Snake. Snake said, "Leopard, I am suffering and feel very ill.

Please examine me." Leopard replied, "You are a dangerous and angry creature, so all the animals fear you. But since you are ill, I shall help." He examined Snake and treated him until he recovered. Snake was very grateful and said, "Ask for anything you desire."

Leopard replied, "I want a beautiful, brightly patterned body." Snake said, "I can change you. I shall bite you, but do not be afraid that you will die. The bites will spread over your body and become spots." Snake bit Leopard, and his whole body became covered with beautiful markings.

They became close friends and have remained so ever since.

Lesson from the Story

Kindness to someone in need can bring unexpected blessings.

New Words

  • Plain: without pattern or decoration
  • Recovered: became well again
  • Markings: patterns or spots on the body

Knowledge Check / Think About It

1. What problem begins Why Leopard Has Spots?

A. Leopard helped sick Snake.

B. Leopard stole Snakes home.

C. Snake chased Leopard away.

Answer: A

2. What choice changes Why Leopard Has Spots?

A. Leopard asked for wings.

B. Leopard asked for horns.

C. Leopard asked for a beautiful patterned body.

Answer: C

3. What lesson can readers learn from Why Leopard Has Spots?

A. Spots are a punishment for honesty.

B. Kindness can bring unexpected blessings.

C. Helping is never rewarded.

Answer: B
