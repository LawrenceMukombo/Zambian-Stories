LANSDOWN COLLECTION 10

Why Fish Live in Water

Long ago fish lived on land with the other animals. They did not live well with their neighbours, for they delighted in mocking the other creatures, comparing one animal with another and causing quarrels. At last all the animals of the forest refused to tolerate their gossip.

They caught the fish and cut out their tongues so that they could no longer make mischief. Then they carried them to the riverbank intending to kill them. During the night the fish slipped into the water and escaped. They decided that it was safer to remain there.

Their wounded tongues became small, so they could no longer gossip. Yet they were happy, because from then on they could not stir up quarrels among the other creatures in the river. That is why fish live in water and do not speak.

Lesson from the Story

Stories can explain the world while teaching careful choices.

Knowledge Check / Think About It

1. What problem begins Why Fish Live in Water?

A. Fish taught birds to sing.

B. Fish caused quarrels through gossip.

C. Fish guarded the forest.

Answer: B

2. What choice changes Why Fish Live in Water?

A. They escaped into the water.

B. They climbed into trees.

C. They hid in a cave.

Answer: A

3. What lesson can readers learn from Why Fish Live in Water?

A. Water teaches animals to speak.

B. Quarrels always help communities.

C. Gossip can destroy peace.

Answer: C
