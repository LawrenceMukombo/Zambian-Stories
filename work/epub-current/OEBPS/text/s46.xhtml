LANSDOWN COLLECTION 6

Why the Hare Drinks Dew

One day Hare was drinking dew from the leaves of a tree. Tortoise, who was nearby, asked, "Why do you do that? Why not drink from the river as the other animals do?" Hare replied, "To tell the truth, I have never thought about it.

Since drinking dew is troublesome, I shall do as you say." He hurried to the river, bent his head and began to drink. Then he saw his own reflection in the water and thought another hare was already there. He drew back angrily and said, "I will never drink from a pool or river again, because my relatives have filled them all."

From that day Hare has kept his promise. He drinks only the morning dew, and his drinking place is still upon the leaves of the trees.

Lesson from the Story

Stories can explain the world while teaching careful choices.

Knowledge Check / Think About It

1. What problem begins Why the Hare Drinks Dew?

A. Tortoise pushed Hare into the river.

B. The river dried up.

C. Hare saw his reflection in the water.

Answer: C

2. What choice changes Why the Hare Drinks Dew?

A. Hare met Lion at the bank.

B. Hare thought another hare filled the river.

C. Hare found honey in the river.

Answer: B

3. What lesson can readers learn from Why the Hare Drinks Dew?

A. Misunderstanding can create needless fear.

B. Dew is stronger than rivers.

C. Reflections are always enemies.

Answer: A
