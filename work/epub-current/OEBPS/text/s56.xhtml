LANSDOWN COLLECTION 16

Why the Giraffe Has a Long Neck

Long ago Giraffe had short legs and a short neck like Rhinoceros. One day Giraffe met his friend Rhino and complained about hunger. Rhino agreed and said, "So many animals feed on the ground that little food remains. The leaves high in the trees are still fresh.

Let us ask the wise Man to help us reach them." They found the Man and explained their trouble. He laughed and said, "That is easy. Come tomorrow at midday and I shall give you special herbs. When you eat them, your legs and necks will grow long enough to reach the treetops."

The next day only Giraffe arrived. Rhino forgot the appointment. The Man gave Giraffe the promised herbs, and when he ate them his legs and neck grew very long. He could now reach the sweetest leaves at the tops of the trees.

Rhino arrived later and was angry to discover that all the medicine had been given to his friend. He believed the Man had deceived him and has remained bad-tempered ever since. Whenever Rhino sees a person, he charges. Giraffe, however, still enjoys the tender leaves that his long neck enables him to reach.

Lesson from the Story

Stories can explain the world while teaching careful choices.

Knowledge Check / Think About It

1. What problem begins Why the Giraffe Has a Long Neck?

A. Both animals refused help.

B. Giraffe came on time for the herbs.

C. Rhino arrived first.

Answer: B

2. What choice changes Why the Giraffe Has a Long Neck?

A. Rhino forgot the appointment.

B. Rhino ate all the herbs.

C. The Man lost the herbs.

Answer: A

3. What lesson can readers learn from Why the Giraffe Has a Long Neck?

A. Forgetting is always rewarded.

B. Anger grows long necks.

C. Keeping appointments can open opportunities.

Answer: C
