The Lazy Brother and the Hardworking Sister

Mwansa and Chanda lived on their family farm. Chanda woke early to plant maize, weed the rows, and water vegetables before the sun grew hot. Mwansa promised to help, but he often found a shady place and fell asleep. 'There is time,' Mwansa said whenever Chanda reminded him. 'The rains have only begun.' But days became weeks, and weeds grew where his maize should have stood. Chanda's field turned green. She worked even when her arms ached, because she knew the harvest would feed them later. Mwansa's field stayed empty except for grass and excuses. When harvest season came, Chanda's baskets were full. Mwansa looked at his bare field and felt shame warming his cheeks. 'Sister, please share,' he said. Chanda shared because hunger should not teach alone, but she also handed him a hoe. The next year Mwansa worked beside her from the first rain to the last harvest. Together they learned that shared work makes shared meals sweeter.

Moral

Hard work feeds tomorrow.
