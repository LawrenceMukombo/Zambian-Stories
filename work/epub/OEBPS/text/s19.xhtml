Bwalya and the Giant Fish

Bwalya lived near the Luapula River and knew its moods like a friend. Some mornings it glittered with fish. Other mornings it gave him only wet nets and tired arms. One bright day his net grew so heavy that he thought it had caught a log. When he pulled it in, a giant fish flashed silver in the sunlight. Its eyes were deep and calm. 'Please set me free,' said the fish. 'Show mercy, and I will remember you.' Bwalya thought of the market, his empty purse, and the roof that needed repair. Then he looked at the fish struggling for breath. He lowered it back into the water. The fish vanished with a splash. The next morning Bwalya found his nets overflowing. He sold many fish, but he did not keep the blessing for himself alone. He repaired the village well, bought books for the school, and helped families whose nets were torn. People said Bwalya became wealthy, but Bwalya knew his true wealth began the moment he chose kindness over hunger.

Moral

Kindness grows when we share its rewards.
