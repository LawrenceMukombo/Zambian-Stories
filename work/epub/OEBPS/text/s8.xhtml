Kalulu Outwits the Leopard

Leopard once chased Kalulu through thorn bushes, around termite mounds, and up a rocky hill. Kalulu's legs were quick, but Leopard's paws were quicker. At last Kalulu reached the edge of a high cliff above a shining river. There was nowhere to run. Kalulu looked down and saw Leopard's reflection trembling in the water. He took one deep breath and pointed. 'Leopard!' he cried. 'Someone below is wearing your spots and stealing your supper.' Leopard leaned over the edge and growled at the reflection. The reflection growled back without making a sound. Leopard bared his teeth. The leopard in the water did the same. While Leopard argued with himself, Kalulu slipped behind a rock, crawled through a narrow crack, and raced down the other side of the hill. When Leopard finally understood, the hare was gone. Kalulu was proud of escaping, but the cliff had frightened him. He promised himself that cleverness should keep him away from danger, not carry him straight to its edge.

Moral

Anger can make even sharp eyes miss the truth.
