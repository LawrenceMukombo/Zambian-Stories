The Ant and the Grain of Maize

During the dry season, Ant carried grains of maize one by one to her tiny storehouse. Each grain was heavy for her, but she did not complain. She climbed, pulled, rested, and began again. Nearby, Grasshopper sang in the sun. 'Why work so hard?' he called. 'The day is bright, and the air is full of music.' Ant smiled but kept walking. 'The rains will come,' she said. 'I am preparing for the days when food is hidden.' Grasshopper danced through the afternoons and slept through the mornings. Ant filled her storehouse little by little. When the first heavy rains fell, the paths turned to mud and the fields became difficult to search. Cold and hungry, Grasshopper knocked on Ant's door. 'Please share,' he said. Ant looked at him for a long moment, then opened the door wide enough for him to enter. 'I will share,' she said, 'but remember this feeling.' Grasshopper ate gratefully and promised that when the sun returned, he would work before singing. Ant nodded, because music is sweeter when tomorrow is not forgotten.

Moral

Hard work and preparation help us through difficult times.
