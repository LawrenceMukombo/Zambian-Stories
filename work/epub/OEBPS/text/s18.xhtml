Nalumino and the Stolen Necklace

Nalumino owned a necklace made of rare blue stones, a gift from her grandmother. She wore it only on special days, and each stone reminded her of a story her grandmother had told beside the evening fire. One day at the river, Nalumino placed the necklace on a flat rock while she washed. A curious monkey dropped from a branch, snatched the necklace, and climbed high into a tree. Nalumino shouted. The monkey chattered. She threw fruit, begged, and sang, but the monkey only held the necklace tighter. The more angry she became, the higher he climbed. At last Nalumino sat in the shade and grew quiet. Tears rolled down her cheeks, not because the stones were costly, but because the necklace carried her grandmother's love. The monkey watched her silence. Slowly he climbed down and placed the necklace in her hands. Nalumino whispered thanks and learned that gentleness can sometimes reach places where anger cannot climb.

Moral

Patience and kindness can reach a stubborn heart.
