Mwanza Learns a Lesson

Mwanza lived in a fine house and wore bright clothes to the village school. His family had many cattle and full grain baskets, and Mwanza liked everyone to know it. When other children brought plain food, he laughed and turned away. One afternoon he walked into the forest to gather wild fruit, still thinking proud thoughts. He followed one path, then another, and soon every tree looked the same. Evening came quickly, and the forest grew cold. Hungry and frightened, Mwanza saw a small hut with smoke curling from its roof. An elderly woman opened the door. She did not ask whether he was rich. She gave him warm porridge, a blanket, and a place beside the fire. 'Why are you kind to me?' Mwanza asked. 'I have not been kind to others.' The woman stirred the fire and said, 'Kindness is a seed. We plant it because the world needs shade.' When Mwanza returned home, his boasting felt heavy in his mouth. He began sharing food, helping younger children, and listening before speaking. Slowly he became rich in the only way that made him beloved.

Moral

Kindness and humility make us truly rich.
