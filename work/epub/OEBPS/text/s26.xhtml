The King Who Had No Child

A wise and kind king ruled a prosperous land. His granaries were full, his people lived in peace, and travelers spoke well of his justice. Yet one sadness sat quietly in his heart: he had no child to inherit the throne. As the king grew older, he wondered who would care for the kingdom when he was gone. At last he called every child to the palace courtyard. He gave each one a seed and said, 'Return in three months with your plant. The child who grows the healthiest one will become my heir.' Liyungu, a humble boy from a poor family, carried his seed home as if it were treasure. He planted it in good soil, watered it each morning, and placed the pot where sunlight could reach it. Days passed. Nothing grew. Other children boasted about green leaves and tall stems. Liyungu's mother touched his shoulder. 'You have done your best,' she said. 'Carry the truth with you.' After three months, the courtyard filled with beautiful plants. Liyungu stood with his empty pot and trembling hands. The king asked why it was empty. Liyungu answered honestly through his tears. The king smiled. 'I gave every child a boiled seed. None could grow. Only Liyungu brought me the truth.' The crowd grew silent, and the king chose Liyungu as heir. Years later, people remembered him as a ruler whose honesty had been planted before his crown.

Moral

Honesty is the foundation of trust.
