Kalaba and the Magic Basket

Kalaba lived with his grandmother in a small hut near the edge of the village. They worked hard, but some evenings the cooking pot held only water and hope. One day Kalaba saw an old woman struggling with a heavy basket. Other children ran past, but Kalaba stopped. He carried the basket all the way to her doorway, though the handle hurt his palms. The woman smiled and gave him a smaller basket. 'When hunger visits,' she said, 'say, Basket, feed us.' Kalaba hurried home. His grandmother listened carefully, then nodded. Kalaba spoke the words, and the basket filled with nshima, vegetables, beans, and fruit. A greedy uncle heard the secret and stole the basket. He shouted the words again and again, but the basket stayed empty. A selfish voice could not wake its magic. Kalaba found the basket later by the road. He and his grandmother used it wisely, sharing with neighbors and wasting nothing. Their hut remained small, but no one left it hungry.

Moral

Blessings stay with generous hearts.
