The Spider and the Honey Pot

Mutale the Spider was clever with plans but weak with heavy things. One morning he found a honey pot wedged high in a tree. The smell was sweet enough to make his legs dance. He tugged and pushed, but the pot would not move. At last he called Antelope, Tortoise, and Bird. 'Help me bring it down,' he said, 'and we will all share.' Antelope shook the branch, Bird loosened the vine, and Tortoise steadied the pot as it rolled to the ground. Everyone smiled, thinking of the feast to come. Then Mutale hugged the pot. 'I made the plan,' he said. 'The honey is mine.' His friends stared at him. Mutale tried to carry the pot away by himself, but it slipped from his legs and smashed on a stone. Honey spread across the dust. Mutale was left with sticky feet and no friends beside him. He learned that a plan is only good when it honors the hands that helped.

Moral

Teamwork brings success; selfishness loses it.
