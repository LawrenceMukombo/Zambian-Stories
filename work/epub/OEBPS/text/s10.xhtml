Tupole and the Magic Drum

Tupole lived in a small village where some seasons were kind and others were difficult. His family did not have much, but Tupole always shared what he could: a cup of water, a bundle of sticks, or a song for someone who felt tired. One day, while fetching water, he saw an old drum floating near the reeds. He pulled it from the river and wiped the mud away. When he tapped it, music rose like sunlight, and bowls of food appeared on the ground. 'This will end hunger in my home,' Tupole whispered. He carried the drum carefully to his mother, and together they shared the food with their neighbors. Soon the chief heard about it and demanded the drum. The chief struck the drum proudly, but nothing happened. He frowned and threw it aside. Tupole picked it up and spoke softly: 'Magic drum, beat only for kind hearts.' The drum answered him at once. From that day Tupole never used the drum for himself alone. Each evening the village gathered, and the first bowl always went to the person who needed it most.

Moral

True magic lives in kindness and sharing.
