Chisanga and the Magic Tree

Deep in the forest stood a baobab tree with leaves that shone like little pieces of morning sun. People whispered that the tree listened to every word spoken beneath its branches. Chisanga, a poor but gentle girl, visited the tree when her family had little food. She did not shout or demand. She placed her hand on the bark and said, 'Wise tree, I seek only your blessing and the strength to help my family.' The golden leaves rustled though no wind was blowing. 'Dig carefully beneath my roots,' said a voice. Chisanga dug with her hands and found a small box of coins and jewels. She used the treasure wisely, buying seed, repairing roofs, and helping neighbors. Her greedy neighbor Luwi heard the story and ran to the tree. 'Give me gold now!' he shouted, kicking the roots. The ground opened beneath him, and he fell into a shallow pit, dusty and embarrassed. Chisanga helped him climb out. Luwi learned that a gift taken with a rude heart becomes a lesson instead.

Moral

Humility is rewarded, while greed leads to loss.
