Kalulu and the Elephant's Challenge

Elephant was the strongest animal in the forest, and he knew it. He pushed fallen trees from paths, lifted heavy logs, and shook fruit from high branches. Soon his strength made him boastful. 'No one can defeat me,' Elephant declared. Kalulu brought a long rope and said, 'Then let us test your strength.' Elephant laughed so loudly that birds flew from the trees. Kalulu tied one end of the rope around Elephant's trunk. Then he carried the other end behind a thicket and tied it firmly around a great old tree. 'Pull when I call,' Kalulu shouted. Elephant pulled. The rope tightened. Leaves trembled. Dust rose around his feet. Still the rope would not move. Elephant pulled until his ears flapped with effort, but the tree held fast and silent. At last Kalulu untied the rope and showed Elephant the trick. Elephant could have been angry, but instead he laughed at himself. 'My strength is large,' he said, 'but your lesson is larger.' From then on, Elephant used his strength with kindness and listened before boasting.

Moral

Strength is greatest when it listens to wisdom.
