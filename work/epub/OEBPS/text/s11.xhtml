The Lost Calf

Mutinta's family owned a small brown calf named Bana Mulenga. He had soft ears, curious eyes, and a habit of wandering whenever a fresh patch of grass called his name. One morning Mutinta was asked to watch him near the fields. At first she watched carefully. Then her friends invited her to play a clapping game under the tree. Mutinta thought, 'Only for a little while.' But a little while stretched like afternoon shadows, and when she turned around, Bana Mulenga was gone. Mutinta searched behind bushes, beside the granary, and along the dusty path. The sun began to sink, and worry filled her chest. She sat under a tree and cried because she knew her family trusted her. An old man passing by listened to her story. 'Dry your tears,' he said. 'Sometimes the thing we seek is found by quiet ears, not running feet.' Mutinta held her breath and listened. Far away came a soft 'Mooo.' She followed the sound to the riverbank, where Bana Mulenga was happily eating grass. Mutinta hugged his neck, led him home, and learned that patience can hear what panic misses.

Moral

Patience and careful listening can lead us home.
