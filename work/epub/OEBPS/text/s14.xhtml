Kachele and the Talking Bird

Kachele was walking through the forest when he heard a tiny voice calling for help. Beneath a thorn bush he found a bird trapped in a hunter's net. Its feathers shimmered blue, green, and gold, but its wings trembled with fear. 'Please set me free,' the bird said. Kachele was surprised, but he did not run away. He knelt carefully and loosened one knot, then another, until the bird could stretch its wings. 'You have a gentle heart,' said the bird. 'Make one wish.' Kachele thought of new sandals, bright cloth, and a wooden cart. Then he thought of his family eating thin porridge at night. 'I wish for enough food to feed my family every day,' he said. The bird sang a clear song, and a basket appeared, filled with maize, pumpkin leaves, groundnuts, and fruit. 'Share with kindness, and it will never run empty,' the bird told him. Kachele obeyed. The basket fed his family and many neighbors, and the village remembered that a small act of mercy can open a large blessing.

Moral

Kindness and generosity bring lasting rewards.
