🥁 CHAPTER FIFTEEN

The Magic Drum

A Story About Kindness, Respect and Sharing

Many years ago, in a small village surrounded by forests and hills, people believed that everything in nature had a lesson to teach.

The rivers taught patience because they travelled slowly but always reached their destination.

The trees taught generosity because they gave shade even to strangers.

The sun taught faithfulness because every morning it returned.

And the elders taught children that kindness was the greatest treasure a person could own.

In this village lived a young boy named Tupole.

Tupole was not from a wealthy family.

His clothes were simple.

His house was small.

His family did not have many cattle or large fields.

But Tupole had something many people admired.

A good heart.

Whenever an elderly person needed help carrying firewood, Tupole helped.

Whenever someone needed water from the stream, Tupole offered.

Whenever younger children struggled, Tupole encouraged them.

Some boys laughed at him.

“Tupole, why do you help everyone?”

“They do not pay you.”

“They do not give you anything.”

Tupole smiled.

“My grandmother says kindness is like planting a tree.”

His friends laughed.

“A tree?”

“Yes.”

“You may not enjoy the shade today, but one day someone will.”

The Long Dry Season

One year, the village experienced a terrible dry season.

The rains delayed.

The streams became smaller.

The fields became dry.

The maize plants struggled to grow.

Food became scarce.

Every family had to be careful with what they had.

Even Tupole’s family struggled.

One evening, Tupole looked at his grandmother.

She gave him her food.

“Grandmother, why are you not eating?”

“I am not hungry.”

But Tupole knew.

She was hungry.

She only wanted him to eat.

That night, Tupole could not sleep.

“I must find a way to help,” he thought.

The Journey into the Forest

The next morning, Tupole woke up early.

He picked up a small basket.

“Where are you going?” asked Grandmother.

“To look for wild fruits.”

“Be careful.”

“I will.”

Tupole walked deep into the forest.

He searched under trees.

He searched near streams.

But he found very little.

The animals were also hungry.

The birds were quiet.

The forest felt different.

After many hours, Tupole became tired.

He sat under a large old tree.

Then he heard something.

A sound.

Very soft.

Boom.

He looked around.

Nothing.

Again.

Boom. Boom.

Tupole stood.

“That sounds like a drum.”

The Strange Discovery

He followed the sound.

Deeper into the forest.

Past tall trees.

Past thick bushes.

Until he reached a small clearing.

In the middle of the clearing was something unusual.

A drum.

It was beautiful.

The wood was smooth.

Traditional patterns were carved around it.

Pictures of animals decorated its sides.

There was:

A lion.

A bird.

A tortoise.

And a hare.

Tupole looked around.

“Who left this here?”

Nobody answered.

Then suddenly...

The drum spoke.

The Talking Drum

“Greetings, Tupole.”

Tupole jumped back.

“Who said that?”

“I did.”

“The drum?”

“Yes.”

Tupole rubbed his eyes.

“Drums cannot talk.”

The drum laughed softly.

“Most drums cannot.”

Tupole slowly moved closer.

“Why are you here?”

The drum replied:

“I have waited many years.”

“For what?”

“For someone with a kind heart.”

Tupole was confused.

“Why me?”

The drum answered:

“Because even when you had little, you still helped others.”

The Drum’s Gift

The drum gave instructions.

“Beat me gently and say:

Drum of kindness, play your song, Bring the help we need along.”

Tupole touched the drum carefully.

He tapped it.

Boom.

Boom.

Then he repeated the words.

Suddenly, the forest became filled with music.

Birds started singing.

The wind moved gently.

Then something amazing happened.

A basket appeared.

Inside was food.

Fresh maize.

Fruits.

Vegetables.

Tupole could not believe it.

“This can help my village!”

The drum replied:

“Remember one thing.”

“What?”

“My gift works through kindness.”

“Never through greed.”

Happiness Returns

Tupole carried the food home.

Grandmother was shocked.

“Where did you find this?”

Tupole explained everything.

The next day, they called the villagers.

They shared the food.

Nobody took too much.

Everyone received enough.

The village became hopeful again.

People sang.

Children laughed.

The elders thanked Tupole.

But not everyone was happy.

The Greedy Chief’s Assistant

The chief’s assistant, Kasonde, heard about the drum.

He became curious.

“A drum that gives food?”

He smiled.

“Why should everyone share?”

“If I get that drum, I can have everything.”

That night, while everyone slept, Kasonde followed Tupole’s footprints.

He found the drum.

“There you are.”

The drum remained silent.

Kasonde picked it up.

“You belong to me now.”

The Wrong Song

Kasonde took the drum home.

He closed the door.

He tapped it loudly.

BOOM! BOOM! BOOM!

“Give me riches!”

Nothing happened.

He hit it harder.

“Give me gold!”

Nothing.

He became angry.

“Stubborn drum!”

Then the drum spoke.

“You have forgotten the lesson.”

“What lesson?”

“A gift taken selfishly loses its power.”

Kasonde ignored it.

He continued beating the drum.

Suddenly...

A strong wind filled the room.

Whoooooosh!

His baskets fell.

His belongings scattered.

Kasonde ran outside frightened.

Returning What Was Taken

The next morning, Kasonde carried the drum back.

Everyone gathered.

He looked ashamed.

“I was greedy.”

“I wanted everything for myself.”

Tupole looked at him.

Then he smiled.

“I forgive you.”

Kasonde was surprised.

“After what I did?”

“Yes.”

“Because everyone can learn.”

The Final Song

Years passed.

The village never forgot the magic drum.

But the greatest lesson was not about magic.

It was about kindness.

The villagers learned to:

Share food.

Help neighbours.

Care for the weak.

Support each other.

One evening, Tupole’s grandmother said:

“The real magic was never inside the drum.”

Tupole looked surprised.

“It wasn’t?”

Grandmother smiled.

“No.”

“The drum only revealed what was already inside your heart.”

And from that day, whenever children heard drums playing in the distance, they remembered the story of Tupole.

The boy who discovered that:

A generous heart creates the greatest music.

🌱 Lesson from the Story

A gift becomes more valuable when it is used to help others. Greed takes away joy, but kindness multiplies blessings.

📚 New Words

  • Generosity – willingness to share with others
  • Scarce – not enough of something
  • Gift – something valuable given freely
  • Selfish – caring only about yourself
