🐇 CHAPTER ONE

Kalulu and the Lion’s Feast

A long time ago, when animals could still talk, there was a great forest near a beautiful river.

The forest was home to many animals.

Lion lived there.

Elephant lived there.

Zebra, Monkey, Buffalo, Giraffe and many others lived peacefully among the trees.

And somewhere near a small hill covered with wild flowers lived a little hare named Kalulu.

Kalulu was famous throughout the forest.

But not always for good reasons.

Whenever something strange happened, everyone asked:

“Where is Kalulu?”

When Hyena lost his food...

They looked for Kalulu.

When Monkey could not find his bananas...

They looked for Kalulu.

When Elephant discovered someone had tied his tail while he slept...

Everyone shouted:

“KALULU!”

One morning, Lion climbed on top of a large rock and gave a mighty roar.

“ROOOOOAAAR!”

The sound travelled across the entire forest.

Birds stopped singing.

Monkeys stopped jumping.

Even Crocodile lifted his head from the river.

“The king has called a meeting!” shouted Elephant.

All animals hurried to Lion’s place.

Lion stood proudly before them.

“My friends,” Lion said, “I have decided to prepare the biggest feast this forest has ever seen.”

The animals cheered.

“There will be fruits!”

Monkey smiled.

“There will be fresh grass!”

Antelope jumped happily.

“There will be honey!”

Bear clapped loudly.

“There will be enough food for everyone!”

The animals celebrated.

Then Monkey raised his hand.

“Your Majesty,” Monkey asked, “what about Kalulu?”

The forest became silent.

Lion frowned.

“No.”

“No?” asked Elephant.

Lion shook his head.

“Kalulu is not invited.”

The animals looked at each other.

“Why not?” asked Zebra.

Lion folded his arms.

“Because whenever Kalulu comes, something strange happens. Last year he convinced Hyena that the moon had fallen into the river.”

Some animals laughed.

“And before that,” Lion continued, “he tricked Leopard into chasing his own shadow.”

More animals laughed.

“This year,” Lion announced, “there will be no tricks.”

Far away behind a tree, two long ears were listening.

It was Kalulu.

He smiled.

“So Lion thinks he can have a feast without me?”

Kalulu laughed quietly.

“We shall see.”

Kalulu Makes a Plan

For two days, all the animals prepared.

Birds decorated trees with flowers.

Elephants carried water.

Monkeys collected fruits.

Everyone was busy.

Meanwhile, Kalulu sat outside his little house thinking.

“How can I enter the feast?”

He thought and thought.

Finally, his eyes became bright.

“I know!”

He found a large empty clay pot.

Then he collected some sweet honey and poured it inside.

After that, Kalulu climbed into the pot.

Only his ears remained outside.

The next morning, the animals saw a strange pot outside Lion’s home.

“What is this?” asked Lion.

A tiny voice came from inside.

“Oh great King Lion! This is special honey sent as a gift.”

Lion smiled.

“I love honey!”

He ordered Elephant:

“Carry this pot inside.”

Elephant lifted the pot.

“This pot feels heavier than honey,” Elephant said.

Inside the pot, Kalulu held his breath.

Lion laughed.

“That means there is plenty of honey!”

The feast began.

Animals danced.

Birds sang.

Everyone enjoyed themselves.

But while they were celebrating...

A small nose appeared from the pot.

Then two ears.

Then a smiling face.

It was Kalulu.

Quietly, he climbed out.

He ate bananas.

He ate sweet fruits.

He tasted the honey.

“This is a wonderful feast,” whispered Kalulu.

“But Lion forgot to invite the most important guest.”

Suddenly Monkey shouted:

“Look! The food is disappearing!”

Everyone turned.

Kalulu quickly jumped back inside the pot.

Lion walked around suspiciously.

“I know this looks like Kalulu’s work.”

He looked everywhere.

Under tables.

Behind trees.

Inside baskets.

But he forgot the honey pot.

Finally, the feast ended.

When everyone slept, Kalulu climbed out and ran home laughing.

The next morning Lion found tiny footprints.

“Kalulu!”

His roar shook the forest.

Far away, Kalulu smiled.

“My friend Lion,” he said, “next time remember: even the smallest animal should not be ignored.”

🌱 Lesson from the Story

Never underestimate someone because they are small or different. Everyone has value.

📚 New Words

  • Feast – a large special meal
  • Clever – able to think quickly
  • Invite – ask someone to come
  • Wisdom – good understanding
